About QuadratNotes

QuadratNotes is a free app for recording seagrass quadrat surveys.

Everything you record stays on this device. There are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

If you tap Get current conditions, your approximate location (to about 1 km) is sent to Open-Meteo to look up the weather and tide. Weather and tide data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).

The app is free and always will be. If it saves you time, you can support its development.

        Export

        Your data is stored only on this phone, in this browser. Export after every survey so you always have a copy.

        What to export

        The quadrats table has one row per quadrat: location, time, conditions, total cover and habitat details. The species table has one row per species per quadrat, with cover, shoots and canopy heights. Join them in R on quadrat_id.

        Share with biodiversity databases?

        This downloads a Darwin Core file to your phone. Nothing is uploaded. You send it on yourself, for example to an OBIS data node such as EurOBIS. You can check the file first with GBIF's free data validator.
