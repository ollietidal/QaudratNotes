Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short: your survey data stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no adverts and no cookies. Something leaves your device only when you choose to export it, send feedback, look up conditions or species names, or open a map.

Who runs QuadratNotes

QuadratNotes is made and run by Oliver R Thomas, who is responsible for the personal data described here (the "data controller"). Contact: hello@quadratnotes.com.

Your survey data

Everything you record (surveys, quadrats, photos, points and notes) is stored only on your device, in your browser's storage. It is never sent to us. Exports and backups are files you create and keep yourself, and you decide where they go. Deleting the app's data, or using "Delete all data on this phone", removes it from your device.

When something does leave your device

WhenWhat is sent, and to whom
You send feedbackWhat you pick and type in the form (your rating, which survey, whether you used it on a real survey, whether you're a student, volunteer, researcher or other, and your message), the app version, your phone type (iPhone, Android or other), and only if you choose to include them, a usage summary (how many surveys and quadrats are on your phone, by habitat, with no locations or survey data) and your email address. It goes through Web3Forms, a form service run by Web3Creative in India, which delivers it to us by email. Nothing from your surveys is sent.
You tap "Get current conditions"Your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) goes to Open-Meteo to look up the weather and tide.
You tap "Check names" in a custom survey template, or download the file for OBIS / GBIFThe species names only (nothing else from your surveys) go to GBIF to check them, or to OBIS and WoRMS to look up their taxon IDs.
You open a map (random or fixed points)Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap's servers, which see your internet address and the area being shown.
You open the websiteThe site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your internet address and basic browser details when the page loads.

We use Google Search Console to see how often the site appears in Google searches. It only reports totals from Google Search; there is no Google code in the app and nothing tracks you on the site.

Feedback: why we keep it and for how long

  • Ratings and messages are used to improve the app. Without an email address they are anonymous, and we keep them for as long as they are useful. If a message happens to include personal details, we remove them.
  • Email addresses are used only to reply to you, and only if you give one. We keep emails that include your address for 12 months after we last contacted each other, then delete them.
  • Web3Forms handles submissions on our behalf under its data processing agreement, passes them on to us, and keeps them only for a short time. Because it is based outside the UK, your feedback is processed there.

The legal basis under UK GDPR is our legitimate interest in improving the app (ratings and messages) and your consent (your email address, which you choose to give and can ask us to delete at any time).

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email hello@quadratnotes.com and we will reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will change too. Significant changes will also be listed in the app's changelog.
