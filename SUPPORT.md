# Getting help with QuadratNotes

## Start here

- **Help guide:** <https://quadratnotes.com/help.html>, step by step, including problems and questions.
- **Export guide:** [docs/DATA-DICTIONARY.md](docs/DATA-DICTIONARY.md), what every exported column means.
- **Changelog:** [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md), what changed in each version.

## Asking a question or reporting a problem

- **GitHub issues** (best for bugs and feature ideas): <https://github.com/ollietidal/quadratnotes/issues>
- **Email:** hello@quadratnotes.com
- **In the app:** the feedback form in the "field testers wanted" panel on the home screen.

Please say which app version you're using (shown in **About**) and on which phone or browser.

## What to expect

QuadratNotes is free and maintained by one person alongside other work. I aim to reply within
two weeks. Problems that could lose or corrupt data, or that stop surveys being recorded or exported,
come first and are usually fixed in the next release. Feature requests are welcome but there is no
promise of when, or whether, they will be added.

There is no paid support or guaranteed availability. For long-term or mission-critical monitoring,
back up after every survey and keep your own copies of exports.

## Security and privacy issues

If you find a security or privacy problem, please email hello@quadratnotes.com rather than opening
a public issue.
